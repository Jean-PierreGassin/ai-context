---
name: write-plan
description: Plan work as a change stack of small, safe ship states. Use when planning, splitting or sequencing multi-step work, or resuming a plan across sessions.
---

Find the smallest coherent ship states that reach the agreed outcome.

1. Read [persisted-plans](references/persisted-plans.md) to continue an existing plan.
2. Investigate the affected areas and settle the facts from evidence.
3. Split the work into checkpoints. Each has one review objective and ships, proves and rolls back on its own.
4. Shape the stack with [change-stack](references/change-stack.md). For a replacement, migration or large refactor, add [change-strategies](references/change-strategies.md).
5. Record the ticket, or why there is none, and choose the execution location with `use-worktrees`.
6. Persist work that spans sessions or a change stack; a contained change stays in the conversation. Then stop for approval.

Keep the plan and its restore context current as work progresses. Take any change to requirements, architecture or stack shape back to the user for approval.
